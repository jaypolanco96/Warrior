# Activity allocation · October 17, 2026

Warrior now includes mature coins by default in its public scan, running desk, Peek and strength comparison. This shares coverage with Laugh's mature list; it does not share wallets, positions or trading locks. Jupiter's broader trending, trading and organic lists can also contribute established coins beyond the explicit list.

`lib/activity.mjs` implements automatic research attention, not an additional profit-learning dial. Every scan measures reported hourly USD buy-plus-sell turnover in its observed universe. Categories are explicit mature coins, pump.fun launches under one hour, other established coins, young coins under five hours, older coins and unknown age (the two mature definitions share one bucket). Token first-pool age is used when available; pool age is the fallback. Mature status is a coverage label, never a safety certification.

Under the current dip presets, 75% of the 36 candle-reading places go first to active dropping coins; remaining places watch other active candidates. Each pool of candidates gets category coverage where space permits, then additional places in proportion to category activity, with a 75% category cap while other categories still have candidates. Candidates within categories rank by turnover, then reported trader count and organic turnover when available. Final entry signals take priority, then activity determines their order. Allocations recalculate from current observations without changing risk, spending limits, stop rules or the four existing adaptive dials.

Liquidity, age, token authority, collapse, minimum volume, entry indicators, executable quotes, costs, reserves and position limits retain their existing gates. Rocket feed observations therefore normally show zero readings under the two-hour minimum pool age. Popularity cannot authorize a purchase or activate the separate Rocket strategy. Discovery category and source metrics are retained on new positions and their sales, including write-offs. Existing trades and shadow scorecards are retained; comparisons spanning this universe change are not evidence that the new policy improved returns.

Order Flow shows observed category shares and read/seen counts without new controls. These are a partial feed sample, not all Solana activity, net SOL inflow or unique human traders. Repeated trading and wash trading can inflate turnover. Missing trader/organic metrics stay unknown. Duplicate mints across overlapping feeds are counted once; the deepest execution pool is retained, Jupiter token-wide activity takes precedence over single-pool activity, and negative authority findings survive merging. Feed failures remain visible. Jupiter lists cache for 45 seconds; launch observations cache for 12 seconds; the existing API edge cache also applies.

Source definitions: [DexScreener API](https://docs.dexscreener.com/api/reference), [Jupiter token information](https://github.com/jup-ag/docs/blob/main/docs/tokens/token-information.mdx), [Jupiter organic score](https://developers.jup.ag/blog/what-is-organic-score). The existing Jupiter datapi list/chart/launch feeds are undocumented and may change; parser failures and source outages must remain honest.

Validation uses synthetic market responses and isolated browser storage. Tests cover mature defaults through the API, deduplication, activity rotation, missing metrics, collapse filtering, no buy from popularity alone, and rocket age rejection. This is a discovery-policy change, not a demonstrated trading edge or a promise of profit.

Current acceptance: `npm test` passed 101 tests; `npm run build` passed; the isolated browser suite passed 236 checks, including populated activity-card containment at nine widths in both themes and the existing tour/funding/security regressions. Phone and desktop activity screenshots were visually reviewed. No real trading or funds movement was used for validation.
