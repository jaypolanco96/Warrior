    How practice and live trading work

    Press Practice buy on any coin, or let Warrior trade the pretend money by itself here.

    For real trading, your Phantom wallet asks you to approve every transaction, so it cannot trade while you are away. Live auto-trading uses a separate small trading wallet that lives in this browser. You move SOL into it once, and Warrior then buys and sells by the rules for as long as this tab stays open.

    Read this first. The trading wallet's key is stored in this browser only, locked with a passphrase you choose. Once you unlock it, a locked copy is also kept on Warrior's server so your other devices can open it with the same passphrase; the passphrase never leaves your device. Still save the backup key: it is the only way back if you forget the passphrase. Anyone with the backup key controls the wallet. Keep only money you are willing to lose in it.

    Coin account rent

    The first time a wallet buys a coin, Solana sets aside about 0.002 SOL as rent for that coin's account. Selling the coin leaves the account empty but still open, with the rent inside. Closing the empty account returns the rent to this wallet. If you buy the same coin again later, the rent is set aside again.

    Only accounts holding exactly zero are closed. This has not been used with real funds yet.

    Sweep winnings home

    After a sale, when nothing is open, Warrior can send the SOL above an amount you choose back to your main wallet. Less money sitting in a browser wallet is safer. It only ever sends to the address shown here, which is set from your connected wallet.

    🚀 Rocket Mode

    The riskiest thing Warrior does. Small bets on pump.fun coins minutes old. Most end at or near zero, and some fall faster than any stop can sell. Treat each bet as money already spent.

    What the Rocket screen refuses

    Every round, while auto-trading is running, Warrior looks at the newest launches and refuses any that show a known warning sign: not a real pump.fun launch, a creator who can print or freeze, a few wallets or the creator holding too much, snipers, insiders or bundled wallets holding too much, too few holders or traders, holders who have already left, a crowd made of bots, a pool too small to get out of, more selling than buying, a copy of a bigger coin, or a creator who launches coins by the dozen and abandons them. Passing that screen does not make a coin safe.

    ⭐ marks a creator with a record: at least three earlier coins, half or more of which graduated from the launch curve. That is a fact about the past. It is not trust, and a graduated coin can still be dumped.

      The rules Warrior follows

      Four signal groups

      Trend: price above the 20 and 50 averages. Momentum: RSI between 50 and 78 with MACD rising. Volume: a surge above average with price over VWAP. Structure: a breakout, or a bounce off the trend line. Three of four must agree, unless you turn the strategy dial.

      Regime and bias

      No entries in a downtrend, in wild chaos, when the hourly bias is bearish, or when a coin is already overbought.

      Risk screen

      At least $40k of liquidity, $100k of daily volume and an hour of pool age (two hours on Chill). No coin that trades on pump.fun's exchange without having been launched on pump.fun, because that pool's money can be withdrawn by whoever put it there. No coins the creator can freeze or mint more of. Coins on the newer token type are traded only when their extras are harmless: none with a transfer fee, code that runs on transfers, a creator who can take or pause coins, or anything Warrior does not recognise.

      Young coins

      A coin with one to five hours of trading is read on one-minute candles instead of five-minute ones. Same four signal groups, the widest stop your rules allow, and half the usual size. Chill leaves young coins alone. Coins under an hour old are never traded: there is not enough to read.

      Rocket Mode

      Off unless you switch it on. Small bets on pump.fun coins one to sixty minutes old that pass a strict screen, sold at the stop, the target, or a trailing stop once the bet has doubled. Most Rocket bets lose.

      Costs

      pump.fun pools charge about 1% each way, so a round trip costs roughly 2% before the price moves at all. Warrior refuses a buy that would cost more than 3% to enter or lose more than 6% on an immediate round trip.

      Position rules

      On the Balanced setting: risk 2% of the wallet per trade, at most 25% in one coin, three positions at once. Stop loss sized from volatility, 10% target, a trailing stop once the trade is up by its own stop distance, a six-hour time limit, and no new trades after losing 10% in a day. Each of those exits can be changed or switched off under Exact numbers. Chill and Spicy change these numbers; the strategy dial shows exactly how.
