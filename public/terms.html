Terms of use

Written in plain words on October 12, 2026. This is a draft by the people who built Warrior. A lawyer has not reviewed it yet; when one does, this line will say so and give the date.

What Warrior is

Warrior is software. It reads public market data, applies a set of published rules, and helps you swap SOL for a coin and back through Jupiter. You can use it with practice money, by approving each trade in your own wallet, or by letting it trade a wallet you create and control.

What Warrior is not

  • Not advice. Nothing on Warrior is financial, investment, tax or legal advice. A signal is a rule-based reading, not a recommendation made for you.
  • Not a broker, an exchange or a bank. Warrior never holds your money or your keys. Trades happen on the Solana network between your wallet and public pools.
  • Not a promise. Warrior does not promise profit, a win rate, or that any feature will work at a given moment.

Your side of it

  • You are an adult where you live, and you are allowed to trade crypto there. Rules differ by country and by state, and Warrior has not yet had a lawyer confirm where it may be offered. Until that is done, checking that you may use it is on you.
  • You decide every trade, including the ones you tell Warrior to make automatically. Turning on auto-trading, an order, slices or copying is your instruction.
  • You keep your passphrase and backup key safe. If they are lost, the wallet cannot be recovered. If someone else gets them, they control the wallet.
  • You pay your own taxes. The trade history can be downloaded to help.
  • You will not use Warrior to break the law, to move money that is not yours, or to attack the site or the services it relies on.

What it costs

Warrior charges no fee today. You still pay pool fees, price impact, slippage and network fees to others; the receipt for each trade lists them. If Warrior ever charges a fee of its own, it will be shown before you trade and on the receipt after, and these terms will change first.

When things go wrong

Warrior is provided as it is. It can have bugs. The services it depends on can fail. Prices can be wrong or late. To the fullest extent the law allows, the people who make Warrior are not responsible for money you lose using it, including losses from bugs, outages, failed or delayed trades, wrong data, or a lost or stolen key. If the law where you live gives you rights that cannot be signed away, you keep them.

Changes

These terms can change. The date at the top changes when they do. Using Warrior after a change means you accept the new terms. Warrior can be changed, paused or shut down at any time; your funds stay in your own wallets either way, and the backup key works in any Solana wallet.
