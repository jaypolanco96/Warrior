The risks, said straight

Written in plain words on October 12, 2026. This is a draft by the people who built Warrior. A lawyer has not reviewed it yet; when one does, this line will say so and give the date.

The short version

You can lose all the money you put into a meme coin, and you can lose it in minutes. Warrior cannot prevent that. It can only make the bets smaller and the exits faster. Nobody at Warrior knows what a coin will do next, and nothing on the site is a promise.

What can go wrong with the coins

  • They can go to zero. Most meme coins do, sooner or later.
  • Rocket Mode is riskier still. It is off unless you switch it on. It bets on coins one to sixty minutes old. Most of them go to zero, some faster than any stop can sell. A creator "with a record" is a fact about earlier coins, not a reason to trust the next one.
  • Young coins are the riskiest. On the Balanced and Spicy settings Warrior trades coins from one hour old, at half size. That first stretch is where the biggest moves and most rug pulls happen. The Chill setting leaves coins under five hours old alone.
  • Rug pulls. A creator or a few large holders can sell everything at once. Warrior checks for some warning signs (a creator who can freeze or print the coin, a very new pool, a thin pool, a few wallets holding most of the supply). Passing those checks does not make a coin safe. On October 12, 2026 a coin passed every check, Warrior bought it with real money, and twenty minutes later its pool was emptied: the position could not be sold at any price. Warrior now refuses that kind of pool, but there are others it cannot see into. For most exchanges the free data does not say whether a pool's money is locked.
  • You may not be able to sell. When everyone runs for the door, the price you get can be far below the price you saw. Warrior refuses trades that would cost too much at that moment, which can also mean a stop does not fill right away.
  • Every trade costs money. pump.fun pools take about 1% each way. Add price impact, slippage and network fees. A trade has to move in your favour just to break even. The receipt shows what each trade cost.

What can go wrong with Warrior

  • Signals are readings, not predictions. An ENTER means recent price and volume met a set of rules. Many ENTER calls lose. The public scorecard shows them all.
  • A stop cannot sell into an empty pool. If the money in a coin's pool is withdrawn, there is nobody to sell to. No stop, at any setting, protects against that. Warrior tells you when it happens.
  • Stops are not guarantees. A stop is checked when Warrior looks, not continuously. A coin can fall through a stop between looks, and the sale then happens lower.
  • It only works while it is running. Auto-trading, orders, slices, your own stops, bedtime rules and alerts work while a Warrior tab is open on your device, or while the terminal bot is running. Close the tab and nothing watches your positions.
  • It depends on other services. Prices come from Jupiter, GeckoTerminal and DexScreener, swaps from Jupiter, and the Solana network carries the trades. Any of them can be slow, wrong or down. When Warrior cannot read something it waits; it does not guess. The status line at the top of the site says when a service is not answering.
  • Software has bugs. Warrior is new. It has been tested with practice money. Read the scorecard and the plan to see exactly what has and has not been tried with real funds.
  • Practice is easier than real. Practice trades use real quotes but are never sent, so they never fail, never slip and pay no network fee. Real trading will be a little worse than practice.

What can go wrong with your wallet

  • The trading wallet lives in your browser. Its key is encrypted with your passphrase and stored on your device. If you forget the passphrase and lose the backup key, the money in it is gone for good. Nobody can reset it, including us.
  • Anyone with the backup key controls the wallet. Never type it into a website, a chat or an email. Warrior will never ask for it, and never asks for your Phantom seed phrase.
  • A compromised device is a compromised wallet. Malware or a bad browser extension can steal a key while it is unlocked. Keep only trading money in the trading wallet, and use the sweep setting to send winnings back to your main wallet.

How to keep the damage small

  • Start in practice mode. It costs nothing.
  • Only put in money you can lose without it changing your month.
  • Leave the Chill or Balanced setting on until you have watched it work.
  • Set a bedtime rule. Let the mood guard talk you out of the trade you would regret.
  • If in doubt, press Get me out.
