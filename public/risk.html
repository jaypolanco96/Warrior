The risks, plainly

Meme coins can lose almost all of their value in minutes. Warrior's agents follow tested rules, but rules are not predictions and no profit is promised. Past results, including the tests behind the rules, do not mean future results.

Practice first. Practice mode uses pretend SOL with real prices and real quotes, so you can watch the impi work before any real money moves.

The trading wallet is yours alone. Its key is made in your browser and locked with your passphrase. Warrior never sees the key or the passphrase. If you lose both the passphrase and the backup key, the wallet cannot be recovered.

Costs are real. Every trade pays network fees, pool fees and price impact. Small trades lose a larger share to fees.

Only trade money you can afford to lose.
